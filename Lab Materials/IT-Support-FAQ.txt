NORTHSTAR IT SUPPORT FAQ
Synthetic training document for the Microsoft Copilot Studio course. Northstar is a fictional company.
Version 2.0. Owner: Northstar IT Service Desk.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the self-service password reset page at https://passwordreset.northstar.example. You need the authentication app on your phone. If you are locked out of the app, call the IT Service Desk.

Q: What are the IT Service Desk hours?
A: Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00 local time in Vancouver and Toronto. Outside these hours, urgent security issues are handled by the on-call engineer through the service desk phone line.

Q: How do I connect to the VPN?
A: Open the Northstar Connect app on your laptop and sign in with your work account. The VPN is required only for the finance system and the file shares; Microsoft 365 works without it.

Q: Is a screen the same as a monitor?
A: Yes. In Northstar requests, "screen" and "display" mean Monitor, "notebook" and "computer" mean Laptop, and "headphones" or "earphones" mean Headset.

Q: Can I cancel an equipment request?
A: Before you confirm a request, you can cancel and nothing is submitted. After a request has been submitted, contact the IT Service Desk and quote the request ID.

Q: How do I check the status of my request?
A: The IT Service Desk emails you when the status changes. You can also ask the service desk and quote your request ID.

Q: Can I install my own software?
A: No. Software is installed from the Northstar Software Center app. If the software you need is not listed, ask the IT Service Desk for a review.

Q: Who do I contact about a lost laptop?
A: Report it to the IT Service Desk within two working days. The service desk informs the Security team.
